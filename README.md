Prompt Engineering & AI-Driven Business Research

Overview

This repository contains learning activities, prompts, examples, and research-verification practices covering Prompt Engineering, Advanced Prompt Engineering, Business Prompt Engineering, and AI-Driven Research with Source Verification and Citations.

The material focuses on using AI effectively for business tasks while keeping human review, verification, and final decision-making at the center.

Context – What is the business situation?

Constraints – What limits must it follow?

Evaluation Checklist

Every important AI response should be reviewed for:

Business Relevance – Does it solve the actual problem?

Accuracy – Are facts and calculations correct?

Feasibility – Can it realistically be implemented?

Cost – Is it financially practical?

Risk – Could it create problems?

Ethics – Could anyone be treated unfairly?

Evidence – Which claims require verification?

Human Judgment – What should the manager decide?

Responsible Use of AI

AI should be treated as an assistant, not as the final authority.

Always:

Verify important facts and calculations independently.

Locate and open original sources before citing them.

Separate facts, assumptions, interpretations, and recommendations.

Do not invent or use fake sources, data, or citations.

Check AI-generated statistics in their original context.

Keep an evidence table for research claims.

Maintain human responsibility for the final report and business decision.

Key Learning

Prompt Engineering should begin with a clearly defined business problem; AI provides input, but managers make the final decision.

Good prompting improves the usefulness of AI output, but human verification and judgment remain essential.
